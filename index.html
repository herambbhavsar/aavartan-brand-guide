02 · Logo

One A. Six lockups.

The logo is the A mark, the AAVARTAN wordmark in Phudu ExtraBold, and the descriptor line. Always use the supplied files. Never rebuild, retype or redraw it.Logo designed by Subhdeep Singh

The idea — hover each line
The A

Aavartan starts with A. The mark is that letter drawn as a peak, the top of a move.

The turn

Āvartan means a turning, a cycle. The cut through the A is one spin across the floor.

The landing

The inner cut is the space a dancer lands in. The negative space does the work.

One violet

#9C33C5 on black, white or paper. No gradients, no second colour inside the logo.

Logo explorer

Choose a lockup and a colour version. Only grounds with enough contrast for that version are offered, and the download is exactly the file on screen.

Clear space

x = one third of the mark's height. Keep at least x free on all four sides: no text, edges, photos or other logos.

Proportion

Wordmark cap height 1.16x · gap mark to word 0.36x · descriptor 0.32x. Measured from the master file, not eyeballed.

Never smaller than this

Shown at actual screen size. Below these sizes the descriptor stops being readable, so switch to the wordmark or the mark instead of shrinking the full logo.
